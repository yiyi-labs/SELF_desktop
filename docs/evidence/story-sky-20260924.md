# SELF origin and star journey: 2026-09-24 tablet audit

Device: physical HarmonyOS tablet `6DP0226117002287`, landscape 3120×2080. The signed HAP was installed with `hdc install -r`; application-private models were not cleared.

## What is implemented

- The origin has five fictional particle memories: one person setting out; tending a plant by a window; crossing an arched bridge and stream; looking toward a distant comet from a hill; and returning beside a sheltered doorway. These are emotional moments, not literal age classifications. The same 4,200 particles disperse as a moving nebula and gather into each scene; only 180 varied stars remain behind the formed scene. Captured photos and personal 3DGS assets are never passed to `NightSkySurface` on the origin.
- The human shapes come from sampled **surface triangles** of a licensed 3D human mesh, not joint lines or a flat icon. The window, plant, bridge, water, hill, comet, doorway, path and canopy are baked as 3D point positions. The five-scene cycle is 220 seconds: each scene rests for 22 seconds, disperses for 7, remains a diffuse nebula for 8 and gathers for 7. Scene titles and two alternating short lines per scene appear with the new gathering. Character action freezes at the start of dispersal while the particles continue to flow outward. The walk animation's last five frames are blended toward its first frame, and particle radius eases from loose to formed perspective; the loose palette and point ordering do not change at a scene boundary.
- A transparent Canvas touch target receives taps and drags without blocking sibling buttons. Within a radius of 9% of the shorter Canvas dimension, nearby particles move toward the finger and some receive subtle glows. The effect fades on release and times out if release is lost. No global particle field is displaced. The settings and ambience card use a cooler moonlit glass tone, with the full settings list visible on the tested tablet.
- These are perspective projections of baked 3D surface positions on an ArkUI Canvas. They have 3D geometry and animated viewpoint, but the runtime Canvas is **not** a native 3D renderer. They are not personal portraits or anatomically accurate reconstructions.
- Personal reconstructions remain in the named star journey. A bounded 3DGS preview is downloaded into the app-private model directory, and the full reconstruction opens on selection. The prior editor remains reachable for future face editing; AI-driven editing is not claimed complete.

## Fictional walking figure asset

- Source: [UMRAM Bilkent `supine-human-model` `assets/human.glb`](https://github.com/UMRAM-Bilkent/supine-human-model/blob/main/assets/human.glb), retrieved 2026-09-24. The repository attributes the original figure to Quaternius and declares both the source and its preparation under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/UMRAM-Bilkent/supine-human-model/blob/main/LICENSE). Source SHA-256: `ce4d543e82e455d5a1253798c9759f12b9eec74929ddbca464447eeaa3a42c5d`. The source itself is not bundled in the app.
- Bake: `scripts/Bake-OriginWalker.mjs` with `three` 0.186.0 reads the `Human_Mesh` surface and its `Walk`, `Working` and `Idle` clips, samples surface triangles by area with a fixed seed, evaluates 20 corresponding frames per scene, normalizes and quantizes positions. All environmental points are generated in the same script. The five 504,016-byte output hashes and frame-loop checks are recorded in `origin-scenes-audit-20260924.json`. The app reads only these point-position assets; source textures and rig are not rendered.
- Limit: the source author describes the base model as low-poly with approximate proportions. The fictional scene is an atmospheric walking silhouette, not a realistic human face or a substitute for the user's personal reconstruction. The generated points do not contain any user's captured data.
