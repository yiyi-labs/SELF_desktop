# SELF origin and star journey: 2026-09-24 tablet audit

Device: physical HarmonyOS tablet `6DP0226117002287`, landscape 3120×2080. The signed HAP was installed with `hdc install -r`; application-private models were not cleared.

## What is implemented

- The origin has two fictional particle memories: a side-on walker, then a person tending a potted plant inside an arched-window room. The same 4,200 particles disperse as a moving nebula and gather into either scene; only 180 varied stars remain behind the formed scene. Captured photos and personal 3DGS assets are never passed to `NightSkySurface` on the origin. The earlier tree, diagonal light stroke and central ring were removed.
- The human shapes come from sampled **surface triangles** of a licensed 3D human mesh, not joint lines or a flat icon. The room's window, plant and floor are modeled with 3D point positions in the offline bake. The 72-second choreography has separate holds, dispersals and reassembly. Character action freezes at the start of dispersal while the particles continue to flow outward. A small, reusable offscreen glow sprite is used only for selected highlights. Turning motion off freezes the scene.
- These are perspective projections of baked 3D surface positions on an ArkUI Canvas. They have 3D geometry and animated viewpoint, but the runtime Canvas is **not** a native 3D renderer. They are not personal portraits or anatomically accurate reconstructions.
- Personal reconstructions remain in the named star journey. A bounded 3DGS preview is downloaded into the app-private model directory, and the full reconstruction opens on selection. The prior editor remains reachable for future face editing; AI-driven editing is not claimed complete.

## Fictional walking figure asset

- Source: [UMRAM Bilkent `supine-human-model` `assets/human.glb`](https://github.com/UMRAM-Bilkent/supine-human-model/blob/main/assets/human.glb), retrieved 2026-09-24. The repository attributes the original figure to Quaternius and declares both the source and its preparation under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/UMRAM-Bilkent/supine-human-model/blob/main/LICENSE). Source SHA-256: `ce4d543e82e455d5a1253798c9759f12b9eec74929ddbca464447eeaa3a42c5d`. The source itself is not bundled in the app.
- Bake: `scripts/Bake-OriginWalker.mjs` with `three` 0.186.0 reads the `Human_Mesh` surface and its `Walk` and `Working` clips, samples surface triangles by area with a fixed seed, evaluates 20 corresponding frames per scene, normalizes and quantizes positions. The indoor window, plant and floor positions are generated in the same script. Outputs: `entry/src/main/resources/rawfile/origin-walker.bin`, 504,016 bytes, SHA-256 `7357941957c5999189822d6010736b3755a63af0fd2f8a211718f63c44a67c64`; and `entry/src/main/resources/rawfile/origin-window.bin`, 504,016 bytes, SHA-256 `39b2755045455cc94ec6680d51baa8ec483d4b6b9c5c49ab6b6fb840eb2d1e35`. The app reads only these point-position assets; source textures and rig are not rendered.
- Limit: the source author describes the base model as low-poly with approximate proportions. The fictional scene is an atmospheric walking silhouette, not a realistic human face or a substitute for the user's personal reconstruction. The generated points do not contain any user's captured data.
